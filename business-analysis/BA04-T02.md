# BA04-T02 — Elicitation and Collaboration | Conducting Elicitation That Surfaces the Real Rule

## ID
BA04-T02

## Chapter
4 — Elicitation and Collaboration

## Live Scenario
Underwriters tell you they 'always' escalate cases above £500,000. Observation shows they also escalate below that when the borrower is in construction, a rule that appears in no policy document and that everyone assumes is written down somewhere.

## The Question
> Which elicitation techniques expose undocumented rules that interviews alone will miss?

## Approach
1. Combine techniques deliberately — interviews for stated process, observation for actual process.
2. Probe exceptions rather than the happy path; the rules live in the exceptions.
3. Use document analysis to find where the stated rule originated and whether it still applies.
4. Replay what you heard back in concrete cases to test whether the rule holds.

## Deliverables
- Elicitation results distinguishing stated from observed practice
- A list of undocumented rules for confirmation

## Tools
- Teams or Zoom for remote sessions
- Miro for live capture
- Word or Confluence for write-up

## Common Pitfall
Trusting the happy path. Stakeholders describe their process as they believe it works; the difference between that and what they do is where the defects come from.

## Interview Angle
Have an example of a rule you found by observation that nobody could produce documentation for. It is a strong story because it is common and rarely told well.
