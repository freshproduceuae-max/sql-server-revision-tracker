# BA03-T05 — Planning and Monitoring | Measuring Business Analysis Performance

## ID
BA03-T05

## Chapter
3 — Planning and Monitoring

## Live Scenario
Your manager asks whether the analysis function is adding value. The only metric available is the number of requirements documents produced, which rewards volume over quality.

## The Question
> What would you measure instead, and how would you avoid a metric that changes behaviour for the worse?

## Approach
1. Measure outcomes of analysis, not volume of artefacts — defect rates traced to requirements, rework after sign-off.
2. Track how many requirements change after approval as a proxy for elicitation quality.
3. Include stakeholder-reported measures, since analysis quality is partly perceived.
4. Review the measures for gaming risk before adopting them.

## Deliverables
- An agreed performance measure set with baselines
- A periodic review of measures for unintended effects

## Tools
- Power BI or Tableau for the dashboard
- Jira for defect-to-requirement traceability

## Common Pitfall
Measuring documents produced or hours logged. Both reward the wrong behaviour and neither correlates with whether the change worked.

## Interview Angle
A strong answer names a metric you rejected and why. It shows you understand that measures shape behaviour.
