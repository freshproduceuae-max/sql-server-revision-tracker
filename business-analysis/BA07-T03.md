# BA07-T03 — Requirements Analysis and Design Definition | Validating Requirements Against Business Need

## ID
BA07-T03

## Chapter
7 — Requirements Analysis and Design Definition

## Live Scenario
A perfectly specified, fully traceable requirements set is delivered on time. Six months after go-live, the process is no faster and the original business case benefit has not materialised.

## The Question
> How does a technically correct requirements set fail to deliver value, and what validation would have caught it?

## Approach
1. Trace each requirement to the business objective and ask whether delivering it moves the measure.
2. Check for the requirements nobody asked for that consumed capacity without serving the objective.
3. Check for the objective with thin requirement coverage — the benefit nobody actually built for.
4. Validate with the benefit owner, not only with the requesting stakeholder.

## Deliverables
- Validated requirements linked to benefit measures
- A list of requirements delivering no measurable benefit

## Tools
- Excel benefit-to-requirement mapping
- Power BI for benefit tracking
- Confluence for validation records

## Common Pitfall
Validating with the person who requested the feature. They will confirm it is what they asked for, which is not the same as it being what the business needed.

## Interview Angle
This is where benefits realisation meets business analysis. Being able to describe a project that delivered on spec and failed on value is a strong, credible story.
