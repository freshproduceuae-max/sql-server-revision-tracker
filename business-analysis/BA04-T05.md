# BA04-T05 — Elicitation and Collaboration | Managing Stakeholder Collaboration

## ID
BA04-T05

## Chapter
4 — Elicitation and Collaboration

## Live Scenario
A key stakeholder who was engaged for three months stops attending. Their input is needed for a decision due in ten days, and the project manager suggests proceeding without them.

## The Question
> How do you diagnose the disengagement rather than escalate straight past it?

## Approach
1. Establish whether it is capacity, relevance or trust — the remedy differs entirely for each.
2. Check that what you are asking of them is something they actually own.
3. Reduce the ask to the specific decision needed rather than a standing meeting commitment.
4. Record the risk formally if engagement cannot be restored in time.

## Deliverables
- A documented engagement risk with mitigation
- A revised engagement approach for that stakeholder

## Tools
- Excel risk register
- Teams or Slack for lightweight asynchronous input

## Common Pitfall
Proceeding without them and calling it decisiveness. If they own the decision, the change will unwind later at far higher cost.

## Interview Angle
This is a behavioural question in disguise. Show diagnosis before escalation — most candidates jump straight to escalating.
