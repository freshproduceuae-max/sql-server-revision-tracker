# BA07-T06 — Requirements Analysis and Design Definition | Analysing Potential Value and Recommending a Solution

## ID
BA07-T06

## Chapter
7 — Requirements Analysis and Design Definition

## Live Scenario
Two options: one costs £2m and delivers £3m of benefit over five years; the other costs £600,000 and delivers £1.2m. The board wants the higher absolute return. Delivery capacity is fully committed for the next year.

## The Question
> How do you frame the recommendation so that constraints and risk are weighed alongside headline return?

## Approach
1. Assess value net of cost, risk and the capacity required to deliver it.
2. Account for time-to-benefit, not just total benefit; delayed value is worth less.
3. Surface the delivery constraint explicitly — a superior option that cannot be resourced is not superior.
4. Recommend clearly, with the conditions under which the recommendation would change.

## Deliverables
- A value analysis covering cost, risk, timing and capacity
- A recommendation with stated conditions and sensitivities

## Tools
- Excel for cost-benefit and sensitivity analysis
- PowerPoint for board presentation

## Common Pitfall
Comparing options on headline benefit alone. Risk-adjusted, capacity-constrained, time-discounted value frequently reverses the ranking.

## Interview Angle
If you have a finance or credit background, this is where it pays. Discounting and risk adjustment are the same reasoning you already know.
