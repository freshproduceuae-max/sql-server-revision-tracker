# BA10-T01 — Techniques | Choosing Elicitation Techniques

## ID
BA10-T01

## Chapter
10 — Techniques

## Live Scenario
You need to understand a claims process spanning four departments, two of which are in another time zone and one of which is openly sceptical about the project.

## The Question
> Which combination of techniques fits these constraints, and what does each one buy you?

## Approach
1. Match technique to constraint — workshops need availability, observation needs access, surveys need scale.
2. Use interviews where a stakeholder is sceptical; a workshop gives them an audience for objection.
3. Use document analysis first to avoid spending scarce stakeholder time on what is already written.
4. Combine techniques so that each covers another's blind spot.

## Deliverables
- A technique selection with rationale per stakeholder group
- An elicitation plan fitting the constraints

## Tools
- Teams or Zoom for distributed sessions
- Miro for asynchronous workshop boards
- Google Forms for scale

## Common Pitfall
Defaulting to workshops for everything. They are expensive, they favour the confident voice, and they are the worst format for a sceptic.

## Interview Angle
Naming three techniques and when each fails is more convincing than listing ten you have heard of.
