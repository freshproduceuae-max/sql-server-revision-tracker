# BA03-T03 — Planning and Monitoring | Establishing Governance for Requirements

## ID
BA03-T03

## Chapter
3 — Planning and Monitoring

## Live Scenario
Three months into a programme, nobody can say who approved a requirement that added £200,000 of scope. Everyone remembers discussing it. No one remembers deciding.

## The Question
> What governance would have made that decision traceable without slowing the team to a halt?

## Approach
1. Define who approves what at which level — not every change needs a board.
2. Set thresholds so small changes move fast and material ones escalate.
3. Require that approvals are recorded against the requirement itself, not in meeting minutes.
4. Define the escalation path for disagreement before you need it.

## Deliverables
- A requirements governance model with approval thresholds
- A change control process with named decision makers

## Tools
- Jira workflow states for approval
- Confluence or SharePoint for sign-off records
- DocuSign where formal signature is required

## Common Pitfall
Governance that treats all changes equally. If a typo correction needs the same board as a scope change, people route around the process entirely.

## Interview Angle
Know the difference between change control and configuration management. Change control governs decisions; configuration management governs versions.
