# BA10-T03 — Techniques | Prioritisation and Decision Techniques

## ID
BA10-T03

## Chapter
10 — Techniques

## Live Scenario
A workshop must choose between five options. Two stakeholders dominate discussion, a third stays silent, and the group converges on the option that the loudest person raised first.

## The Question
> Which decision techniques counteract group dynamics rather than amplifying them?

## Approach
1. Collect individual positions before group discussion to prevent anchoring.
2. Use structured scoring against agreed criteria rather than open debate.
3. Make the criteria and weights explicit and agree them before seeing the options.
4. Record dissent rather than manufacturing consensus, so a later reversal is not a surprise.

## Deliverables
- A decision with recorded criteria, scores and dissent
- An auditable rationale for the chosen option

## Tools
- Excel for weighted scoring
- Miro for anonymous input
- Jira or Trello for MoSCoW backlog views

## Common Pitfall
Open discussion before individual input. The first opinion voiced anchors the group, and the quietest stakeholder often holds the most operationally important objection.

## Interview Angle
Anchoring is a real and nameable effect. Referring to it explicitly shows you have thought about facilitation, not just attendance.
