# BA09-T02 — Underlying Competencies | Behavioural Characteristics Under Pressure

## ID
BA09-T02

## Chapter
9 — Underlying Competencies

## Live Scenario
In a steering meeting a director states that the analysis is wrong and the requirement was always agreed. You have documentation showing otherwise. The room is watching.

## The Question
> How do you handle this so the decision is corrected without turning it into a personal contest?

## Approach
1. Separate the factual correction from the interpersonal challenge and address the fact only.
2. Offer the evidence neutrally rather than as proof of who was right.
3. Move the resolution offline if the meeting is not where it can be settled.
4. Follow up in writing so the record is corrected regardless of what was said in the room.

## Deliverables
- A corrected record circulated after the meeting
- A resolved position agreed with the stakeholder

## Tools
- Confluence or email for the written record
- Jira history as the evidence trail

## Common Pitfall
Winning the exchange in the room. You may correct the record and lose the working relationship, which costs more across the rest of the programme.

## Interview Angle
This is a behavioural question you will certainly meet. The strong answer corrects the fact and protects the relationship — both, not one.
