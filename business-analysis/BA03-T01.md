# BA03-T01 — Planning and Monitoring | Choosing a Business Analysis Approach

## ID
BA03-T01

## Chapter
3 — Planning and Monitoring

## Live Scenario
You are handed two projects on the same day. One replaces a core banking ledger under regulatory deadline. The other is a customer-facing mobile feature the product team wants to test in six weeks.

## The Question
> Why should these two projects not use the same analysis approach, and how do you justify the difference to a governance board that wants consistency?

## Approach
1. Assess each on change complexity, regulatory exposure and cost of being wrong.
2. Choose predictive analysis where rework is expensive and requirements are stable — the ledger.
3. Choose adaptive analysis where learning is cheap and requirements are uncertain — the mobile feature.
4. Document the rationale so the choice is defensible rather than stylistic.

## Deliverables
- A documented analysis approach per project with rationale
- Agreed deliverables and level of formality for each

## Tools
- Confluence for the approach document
- Microsoft Project or Smartsheet where predictive

## Common Pitfall
Letting the organisation's default methodology decide for you. A regulated ledger migration run on two-week discovery cycles produces an audit finding, not agility.

## Interview Angle
Expect 'are you agile or waterfall?' The strong answer refuses the framing and explains what drives the choice.
