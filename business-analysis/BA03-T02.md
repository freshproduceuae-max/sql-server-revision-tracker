# BA03-T02 — Planning and Monitoring | Planning Stakeholder Engagement

## ID
BA03-T02

## Chapter
3 — Planning and Monitoring

## Live Scenario
A finance transformation has 40 identified stakeholders. The previous analyst invited all 40 to every workshop. Attendance collapsed to six, and the six were the least affected people.

## The Question
> How do you design an engagement plan that gets the right people in the room without consuming the organisation's time?

## Approach
1. Segment by influence and interest, then assign an engagement mode to each quadrant.
2. Reserve workshops for those who must co-create; use interviews for high-influence, low-availability people.
3. Use written review cycles for those who only need to be informed.
4. Set decision rights explicitly — who is consulted versus who approves.

## Deliverables
- A stakeholder engagement plan with mode and frequency per group
- A RACI showing decision rights

## Tools
- Excel or Sheets for the RACI
- Teams or Outlook for the cadence
- Trello or Asana for tracking actions

## Common Pitfall
Confusing consultation with approval. Stakeholders who believe they are approving when they are only being consulted will block at the worst moment.

## Interview Angle
Be ready for 'how do you deal with an unresponsive stakeholder?' Escalation is part of the answer, but so is checking that you asked them for something they actually own.
