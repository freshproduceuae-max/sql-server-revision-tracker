# BA06-T03 — Strategy Analysis | Assessing Risks to the Change

## ID
BA06-T03

## Chapter
6 — Strategy Analysis

## Live Scenario
A migration business case lists one risk: 'project delay'. Midway through, the vendor is acquired, two key staff leave, and a regulatory change alters the data retention requirement. None were considered.

## The Question
> How do you assess risk in a way that surfaces the threats that actually materialise?

## Approach
1. Assess risk across categories — delivery, operational, regulatory, vendor, people — rather than as a single list.
2. For each, state the impact on the value expected from the change, not just on the schedule.
3. Assign owners; an unowned risk is an observation.
4. Distinguish risks you will mitigate from those you accept, and record the acceptance.

## Deliverables
- A risk register with categories, owners and responses
- Recorded risk acceptances with rationale

## Tools
- Excel risk register
- Jira for mitigation actions
- Power BI for risk reporting

## Common Pitfall
A risk register written once at initiation and never revisited. Risks change as the change progresses; a static register is documentation theatre.

## Interview Angle
Know the difference between a risk and an issue. A risk has not happened yet; an issue has. Candidates confuse them constantly.
