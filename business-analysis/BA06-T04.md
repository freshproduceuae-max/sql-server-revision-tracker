# BA06-T04 — Strategy Analysis | Defining the Change Strategy

## ID
BA06-T04

## Chapter
6 — Strategy Analysis

## Live Scenario
A bank must migrate 400,000 accounts to a new platform. The options are a single cutover weekend, a phased migration by product, or running both platforms in parallel for six months.

## The Question
> How do you evaluate these options on more than cost, and make the trade-off explicit for the board?

## Approach
1. Define the evaluation criteria before evaluating — risk, cost, customer impact, reversibility.
2. Assess each option against all criteria rather than defending a preferred one.
3. State the transition requirements each option creates, since these are often the deciding cost.
4. Recommend one option with the trade-off stated plainly, not buried.

## Deliverables
- A change strategy with options assessed against stated criteria
- Transition requirements per option

## Tools
- Excel for cost-benefit and option scoring
- PowerPoint for the board decision
- Visio for transition state models

## Common Pitfall
Presenting one option with two strawmen. Boards recognise it, and it destroys the analyst's credibility for every future recommendation.

## Interview Angle
Reversibility is an underrated criterion. Mentioning it signals you have thought about what happens when the change goes wrong.
