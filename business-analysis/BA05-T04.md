# BA05-T04 — Requirements Life Cycle Management | Assessing Requirements Changes

## ID
BA05-T04

## Chapter
5 — Requirements Life Cycle Management

## Live Scenario
Late in build, the head of operations asks for an extra approval step. It sounds minor. It touches the workflow engine, the audit log, three reports and the user training already written.

## The Question
> How do you assess this change so the decision is made on real cost rather than on how small it sounds?

## Approach
1. Trace the change through the requirements to find everything it touches.
2. Assess impact on cost, timeline, risk and already-delivered work including training and documentation.
3. Check the change against the original business need — does it still serve it, or is it scope creep with a sponsor.
4. Present options rather than a single verdict: accept, defer, or accept with a named trade-off.

## Deliverables
- A change impact assessment with options
- An updated traceability view showing affected artefacts

## Tools
- Jira for the change request
- Excel for impact analysis
- Confluence for the decision record

## Common Pitfall
Assessing only the build effort. Training, documentation, migration and reporting are where late changes actually cost, and they are usually invisible to the requester.

## Interview Angle
Expect a scenario question on late change. Traceability is what makes impact assessment possible — say so.
