# BA04-T03 — Elicitation and Collaboration | Confirming Elicitation Results

## ID
BA04-T03

## Chapter
4 — Elicitation and Collaboration

## Live Scenario
Two weeks after a workshop, you circulate notes. The operations lead replies that half of what you captured is not what they meant, and the build has already started against your version.

## The Question
> How do you confirm understanding early enough that a misreading is cheap rather than expensive?

## Approach
1. Confirm within the session by replaying understanding in the stakeholder's own terms.
2. Cross-check statements from different stakeholders against each other and surface conflicts explicitly.
3. Use a model or example rather than prose — people spot errors in a diagram or a worked case that they miss in paragraphs.
4. Timebox confirmation so silence does not become implicit agreement forever.

## Deliverables
- Confirmed elicitation results with recorded conflicts
- A log of assumptions awaiting validation

## Tools
- Lucidchart or Visio for confirming models
- Confluence comments for asynchronous review

## Common Pitfall
Treating no response as agreement. It usually means nobody read it, and the disagreement arrives later at a much higher cost.

## Interview Angle
Expect 'what do you do when two stakeholders disagree?' Confirmation is where you find it; governance is where you resolve it.
