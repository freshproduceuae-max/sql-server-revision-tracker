# BA07-T02 — Requirements Analysis and Design Definition | Verifying Requirements

## ID
BA07-T02

## Chapter
7 — Requirements Analysis and Design Definition

## Live Scenario
A requirements set passes review and goes to build. During development the team finds two requirements that contradict each other and one that cannot be tested as written.

## The Question
> What verification would have caught these before the build started?

## Approach
1. Check each requirement for the quality characteristics — unambiguous, testable, complete, consistent, feasible.
2. Check the set as a whole for internal contradiction, which single-requirement review never finds.
3. Confirm every requirement is atomic; compound requirements hide contradictions inside themselves.
4. Review with someone who will have to test it, since testability failures are invisible to authors.

## Deliverables
- Verified requirements meeting agreed quality criteria
- A defect log of requirements returned for rework

## Tools
- Confluence review workflow
- Jira for requirement defects
- Excel checklist for quality criteria

## Common Pitfall
Confusing verification with validation. Verification asks whether the requirement is well written; validation asks whether it is the right requirement. Both are needed and they catch different failures.

## Interview Angle
The verification versus validation distinction is asked constantly. Have a one-line answer and an example of each.
