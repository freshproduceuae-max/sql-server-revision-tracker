# BA05-T05 — Requirements Life Cycle Management | Approving Requirements

## ID
BA05-T05

## Chapter
5 — Requirements Life Cycle Management

## Live Scenario
A requirement was 'approved' in a meeting. Build proceeded. At UAT the approver states they had approved the concept, not the detailed behaviour, and the behaviour is wrong.

## The Question
> How do you make approval unambiguous about what exactly is being approved?

## Approach
1. State explicitly what is in scope of the approval and what is not.
2. Approve against a specific version, not a moving document.
3. Record approval against the requirement itself so it survives the meeting.
4. Confirm the approver has the authority — approval by someone without decision rights is not approval.

## Deliverables
- Recorded approvals tied to requirement versions
- A named approver per requirement area

## Tools
- Jira approval workflow states
- DocuSign for formal sign-off
- Confluence page versioning

## Common Pitfall
Approval recorded in meeting minutes. Minutes describe a discussion; they do not tie a decision to a specific version of a specific requirement.

## Interview Angle
Sign-off disputes are common in interviews. Version-specific approval is the answer that shows you have been burned before.
