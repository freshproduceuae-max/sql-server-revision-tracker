# BA08-T04 — Solution Evaluation | Assessing Enterprise Limitations

## ID
BA08-T04

## Chapter
8 — Solution Evaluation

## Live Scenario
A new CRM works exactly as designed. Adoption sits at 30% because the sales incentive scheme still rewards the behaviour the old process encouraged, and nobody changed it.

## The Question
> How do you identify limitations that sit outside the solution but determine whether it delivers value?

## Approach
1. Examine the surrounding operating model — incentives, roles, policies, culture — for conflict with the intended behaviour.
2. Check whether the change assumed an organisational change that was never scoped or owned.
3. Identify who owns each blocking factor, since none of them are owned by the project.
4. Escalate as a benefit risk rather than a project defect, because the fix is outside project control.

## Deliverables
- An enterprise limitation assessment with owners outside the project
- A benefit risk escalation

## Tools
- Excel for the limitation register
- PowerPoint for the sponsor escalation
- Miro for operating model mapping

## Common Pitfall
Assuming behaviour change follows system change. Incentives beat interfaces every time; if the scheme rewards the old way, the old way persists.

## Interview Angle
This distinguishes senior from junior answers. Junior candidates fix the system; senior candidates ask why people are not using it.
