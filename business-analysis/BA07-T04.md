## Chapter
7 — Requirements Analysis and Design Definition

## Live Scenario
A programme has 600 requirements across five workstreams. Each workstream's set is coherent internally. Nobody can tell whether they are consistent with each other, or whether the whole covers the business need.

## The Question
> How do you structure a large requirements set so that completeness and consistency can be assessed at all?

## Approach
1. Organise requirements into a structure that mirrors the business, not the project team layout.
2. Define the relationships between requirement sets, especially where workstreams interface.
3. Use the structure to test coverage — every business objective should map to a populated area.
4. Identify the interface requirements explicitly; the gaps between workstreams are where integration defects live.

## Deliverables
- A requirements architecture showing structure and relationships
- An identified set of cross-workstream interface requirements

## Tools
- Lucidchart or Enterprise Architect for structure
- Jira epics and links
- IBM DOORS for large sets

## Common Pitfall
Structuring requirements by delivery team. Teams reorganise; the business structure is more stable and makes coverage assessable.

## Interview Angle
Relevant for any large-programme role. The insight to lead with is that defects concentrate at the interfaces between workstreams.
