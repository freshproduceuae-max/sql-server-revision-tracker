# BA08-T01 — Solution Evaluation | Measuring Solution Performance

## ID
BA08-T01

## Chapter
8 — Solution Evaluation

## Live Scenario
Six months after go-live, the sponsor asks whether the new system delivered. The team reports 99.8% uptime and 40 defects closed. The sponsor asks whether processing time fell, and nobody has measured it.

## The Question
> How do you define performance measures that answer the business question rather than the technical one?

## Approach
1. Derive measures from the business objectives stated at the outset, not from what is easy to instrument.
2. Include the baseline so the measure has something to be compared against.
3. Cover both intended benefit and unintended effect — throughput may rise while quality falls.
4. Agree the measures before go-live; defining them afterwards invites selection to fit the result.

## Deliverables
- An agreed performance measure set tied to business objectives
- A measurement plan with owners and frequency

## Tools
- Power BI or Tableau for dashboards
- Excel for baseline comparison
- SQL for extracting operational measures

## Common Pitfall
Measuring what the system reports rather than what the business needed. Uptime is a technical measure; it tells you nothing about whether the process got faster.

## Interview Angle
A strong story here is a project that hit every technical measure and missed the business one. It shows you evaluate rather than just deliver.
