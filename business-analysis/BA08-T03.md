# BA08-T03 — Solution Evaluation | Assessing Solution Limitations

## ID
BA08-T03

## Chapter
8 — Solution Evaluation

## Live Scenario
Users are bypassing the new system for 20% of cases, using a spreadsheet instead. The system is functioning as specified. The workaround is undocumented and growing.

## The Question
> How do you assess whether the limitation is in the solution or in how it is being used?

## Approach
1. Investigate the bypassed cases specifically — what do they have in common.
2. Establish whether the solution cannot handle them or merely handles them badly.
3. Check whether the case type was in scope originally; a known exclusion is different from a defect.
4. Quantify the risk the workaround creates, since undocumented spreadsheets carry control and audit exposure.

## Deliverables
- A solution limitation assessment distinguishing defect from exclusion
- A quantified risk statement for the workaround

## Tools
- SQL or Excel for analysing bypassed cases
- Jira for raised defects
- Visio for the actual versus intended process

## Common Pitfall
Treating a workaround as a training problem. Users usually work around a system because it genuinely does not fit the case, and blaming them prevents you from learning that.

## Interview Angle
Workarounds are excellent interview material because they are universal. The insight is that they are evidence, not misbehaviour.
