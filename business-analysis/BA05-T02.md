# BA05-T02 — Requirements Life Cycle Management | Maintaining Requirements for Reuse

## ID
BA05-T02

## Chapter
5 — Requirements Life Cycle Management

## Live Scenario
Three projects in the same organisation each write their own definition of 'active customer'. The definitions differ subtly. Two years later, three reports disagree and nobody can say which is correct.

## The Question
> How do you maintain requirements so that a shared definition is stated once and reused rather than rewritten?

## Approach
1. Identify requirements that describe enduring business rules rather than project-specific behaviour.
2. Move those to a maintained rule catalogue owned outside the project.
3. Reference the catalogue from projects instead of copying the text.
4. Assign ownership for review, since an unowned catalogue decays into another stale document.

## Deliverables
- A business rule catalogue with named owners
- Project requirements referencing rather than duplicating rules

## Tools
- Confluence for the catalogue
- Jira links for references
- Excel for the rule inventory

## Common Pitfall
Copying a rule into a project document. The copy immediately becomes a second source of truth and drifts from the original.

## Interview Angle
This maps directly onto master data management. If you know that domain, the parallel is worth drawing explicitly.
