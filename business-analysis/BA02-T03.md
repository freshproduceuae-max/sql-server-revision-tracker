# BA02-T03 — Key Concepts | Identifying Stakeholders You Would Otherwise Miss

## ID
BA02-T03

## Chapter
2 — Key Concepts

## Live Scenario
A hospital rolls out a new patient scheduling system. Clinicians and administrators were consulted throughout. On go-live, the medical records team discovers the system does not produce the audit trail their regulator requires.

## The Question
> What systematic method would have surfaced the medical records team before go-live rather than after?

## Approach
1. Work outward from the process, not the org chart — trace who touches the data before, during and after.
2. Include regulatory and audit roles explicitly; they rarely self-identify as users.
3. Include those who receive outputs downstream, even if they never log in.
4. Classify each by influence and interest so engagement effort is proportionate.

## Deliverables
- A stakeholder register including non-user stakeholders
- An influence/interest grid driving engagement frequency

## Tools
- Excel or Sheets for the stakeholder matrix
- Visio or Lucidchart for the process trace

## Common Pitfall
Building the stakeholder list from the org chart. The org chart shows reporting lines, not information flows, and regulators never appear on it.

## Interview Angle
A very common question is 'how do you handle a stakeholder you discover late?' The answer involves impact assessment and change control, not heroics.
