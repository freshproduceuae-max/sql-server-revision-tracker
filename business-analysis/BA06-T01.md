# BA06-T01 — Strategy Analysis | Analysing the Current State

## ID
BA06-T01

## Chapter
6 — Strategy Analysis

## Live Scenario
A manufacturer wants to 'digitise procurement'. Nobody can say how long the current process takes, how many people touch a purchase order, or what it costs per order. The improvement target of '50% faster' has no baseline behind it.

## The Question
> How do you establish a current state description solid enough that improvement can actually be measured against it?

## Approach
1. Map the process as it is performed, not as policy describes it.
2. Quantify the baseline — cycle time, volume, cost, error rate — even approximately.
3. Identify the constraints causing the pain, separating symptoms from causes.
4. Capture the current-state capabilities, since the change will build on or replace them.

## Deliverables
- A current state assessment with a quantified baseline
- A process model reflecting actual practice

## Tools
- Visio, Lucidchart or Bizagi for process models
- Excel for baseline metrics
- SWOT in PowerPoint for context

## Common Pitfall
Skipping the baseline because it is hard to measure. Without it, no future benefit claim can be substantiated and the business case is unfalsifiable.

## Interview Angle
Expect 'how do you measure success?' The honest answer starts with whether a baseline exists at all.
